About us

More than a haircut — a ritual.

At Hugi Barbershop we combine the classic art of barbering with contemporary technique. Our barbers specialise in modern fades, sharp beard trims and clean precision cuts — with the hot towel, the small talk and the careful finish that make a barbershop worth returning to. Whether it's a quick touch-up or a full grooming session, you leave looking sharper than you arrived.
